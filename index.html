How to play & edge cases

  • Easiest: click or tap the board where you want the snake to go.
  • Keyboard: ← / → or A / D turn the snake (it always moves forward). Tap the board, swipe, or the ◀ ▶ buttons work too.
  • Eat glowing orbs to grow and score. The snake speeds up as you eat.
  • Chain eats within 5s for a combo multiplier (up to x5). Pink bonus orbs are worth 50 × combo — grab them fast!
  • Solid walls kill — or enable Wrap walls to pass through.
  • Every 6 foods you level up — and in Obstacles mode each level adds deadly blocks. Each level brings a new arena: 🌱 Meadow → 🏜️ Desert → 🌊 Ocean → 🌋 Volcano → 🌌 Space → 🌲 Forest → 🌅 Sunset → ❄️ Ice.
  • Pick a mode above: Classic walls kill, Wrap passes through, Obstacles adds hazards.
  • Quick opposite turns are ignored so you can't instant-suicide by double-tap.
  • Game auto-pauses when the tab hides or window blurs.
  • Fill the whole 20×20 board to win (400 segments).
  • Dying at level 2+ offers ⭐ Prestige: restart with a permanent +10% score bonus that stacks forever.
  • The zoom is fixed for your screen, so it never pumps while you play. Wheel / pinch zooms freely for a few seconds, then framing resumes. On a phone the camera zooms in until the board is readable and follows the snake, with a dashed outline marking the walls that run off-screen.
  • Terrain rules: 🧊 Ice is slippery (turns land one cell later), 🏜️ Desert food withers in 20s, 🌋 Volcano embers telegraph orange before they burn.
  • Obstacle layouts are hand-designed and symmetric per arena (a scatter variant appears sometimes); fresh blocks pulse orange before they can kill.
  • A cyan 🛡 shield orb sometimes appears — grab it to survive your next crash.
  • Unlock snake skins with achievements (see the Look & feel settings). Links like ?theme=volcano&seed=7 replay the same map and spawns.
  • 📅 Daily challenge: the same map and the same twist for everyone, all day. Everyone gets an identical seed — chase a friend's score.
